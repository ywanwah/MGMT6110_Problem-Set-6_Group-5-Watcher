One named catch

Amazon's operating-income guidance was missing, and the assessor scored Complete 5/5

Jo, 26 October, reading exhibit 99.1 of accession 0001018724-26-000024. The release's outlook section guides Q3 net sales ($197.0–202.0bn) and Q3 operating income ($22.5–26.5bn) and flags an unfavourable FX impact of about 80 basis points. The watcher recorded net sales only; the assessor rated S2 Complete at 5 because what was there was quoted word for word. The same pattern appears on all four dashboards.

One disagreement and its resolution

Jo versus the assessor on Amazon's S2

The assessor gave 5; Jo gave 3. Resolved by going back to the release: the guided figure exists and is absent from the brief, so the form carries S2 = 3 and the proposed change to the assessor is to re-read the outlook section before scoring Complete. A second, cross-dashboard disagreement: two watchers labelled Meta's tone positive and two balanced; the assessors that saw “positive” ruled it not supported, since reported operating income fell 36.8% after a $9.89bn legal charge, so balanced stands.

05 · Oversight · shared 3-minute discussion

Six structural gaps, with their true counts

Three appear on all four dashboards, one on three, two on two. Drag the cube or use the buttons; the panel names the dashboards affected and the evidence.

The duty check a person still runs, 7:15 AM

    What the agents are good at, and blind to

    Good at: every confirmed figure equals the 10-Q to the last digit; units in thousands are converted; “cannot check” is used rather than guessed; the assessor overruled two tone calls and flagged Meta's “investments are paying off” on all four dashboards.

    Blind to: what is absent. A second guidance line, a bank's revenue components, a filing that was never an earnings release, a cache filled the previous evening. The assessor rates the watcher's output, not the world, so a person reads the outlook section and the sources line before a figure is used.

    06 · Backup · How the tool works

    Six layers between EDGAR and the analyst

    Schedule

    Watcher 6:07 AM, assessor 6:37 AM, Singapore time, daily. That is 6:07 PM in New York under daylight time and 5:07 PM once US clocks change on 1 November.

    Rules of the check

    A figure is confirmed when the quote holds it and the 10-Q value is within 0.5%; EPS must match to the cent. Anything unconfirmed is re-checked each morning for 10 days.

    Sources

    EDGAR full-text search (8-K item 2.02), the submissions API for the watchlist, the companyfacts XBRL API for filed values. No other data source, no connectors, nothing sent.

    09 · Backup · Proposed operating controls

    What we would put in place if the tool were adopted

    Optional improvements proposed by Group 5. They are not PS6 submission requirements, and nothing here is conditional on them.

    Operating mode

    1. Human-in-the-loop through the Q3 reporting season: no figure from the page reaches a meeting note without the 7:15 duty check.
    2. Seven fixes below, the two plumbing fixes first.
    3. Analyst-optional mode only after the six gate conditions hold for ten consecutive trading mornings.
    4. Scope stays at reporting and checking; briefs are screened weekly for buy, sell or sizing language.

    Seven fixes

      Readiness gate

      Tick a condition when it has held for ten consecutive trading mornings. Remembered in this browser only.

        Bank metrics

        Keep the filing's labels. For a bank, show net interest income and noninterest income as filed; if a total revenue line is added it is analyst-calculated (NII plus noninterest income) and labelled so; income before income taxes is a filed label and is not operating income. First Hawaiian: NII $174.1m, noninterest income $56.1m, income before taxes $49.9m. BancFirst: NII $136.0m, noninterest income $51.4m, income before taxes $86.1m.
